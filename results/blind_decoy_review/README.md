# Blinded decoy review

Open the numbered image pages and record one decision for each structure in
`blind_review_sheet.csv`:

- `accept`: plausible FLP-like candidate;
- `reject`: not a plausible FLP-like candidate;
- `uncertain`: cannot be decided from the 2D structure alone.

Do not open `blind_review_answer_key.csv` until every decision has been recorded.
The panel contains model candidates and chemically valid decoys in random order.

The final study endpoint is boron-centered. Al/Si-only entries `B007`, `B034`
and `B042` are retained in the full-panel result but marked as outside the
primary scope in `blind_review_scored.csv`. Both full-panel and B-centered
metrics are reported in `blind_review_metrics.csv`.
